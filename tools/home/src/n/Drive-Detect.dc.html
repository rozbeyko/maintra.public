On the road?

What sets it offThe phone’s motion sensor says “in a vehicle” for 2 minutes. No location for this: iPhone keeps that history on the phone; on Android it comes from Google Play services.
With Maintra OBDThe adapter sees the engine start, so there is no guessing and no question: the car is known. It can start counting by itself if you let it.
Not meYou’re a passenger, in a taxi, on a bus. Quiet for the rest of this drive; the sensor can’t tell whose car it is.
Other carPick Monster or any car in the garage. The km go there.
Once per driveNever twice in one drive, never while a trip already runs. Off in Map settings with one switch.
