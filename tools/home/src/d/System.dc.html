Calm garage, loud moments

Principles
01One family, two voicesFira Sans for reading, Tektur for the game voice: names, numbers, labels, tabs. Tektur has the same chamfers as our corners and icons. Both cover Cyrillic and ₴. Numbers are always tabular.HIG: two typefaces, both with Cyrillic and ₴
02Controls lean, data stands straightButtons, segments, tabs and separators share the slant. Chart bars are straight, gauges are rev-counter ticks. Only status dots are round.
03Content is matte, what floats is glassTray, + menu, sheets and the buttons over a photo are glass. Rows and lists are matte. Never glass on glass.HIG Liquid Glass: navigation layer only
04One gold thing to press per screenRed is late, amber is soon, green is saved or fine. Nothing else gets colour.
05One line per thingName left, the one number that matters right. Hairlines and space group things, not cards.Flighty, ADA winner
06Calm by default, loud at the momentStamps, +8 and big italic headlines live in Moments only.NFS Unbound
07Titles: big on tabs, small when pushedGarage, Places, Stats, Profile get a big title. Pushed screens put the title in the top bar, centred, next to ‹ back.HIG large vs inline titles
08+ is for what the screen can’t reachScan a receipt (AI decides: workshop or fuel), Service, Fuel, Car. Four things, never a list.
09The garage is yoursThe polaroid always stays. What sits under it is picked in Edit garage: health, up next, this month, shelf, fuel, or nothing.
