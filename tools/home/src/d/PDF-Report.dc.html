For a buyer

Paper, not the app: black on white, prints fine in grey. Units and labels in the owner’s language. Shared through the system sheet, works offline.

Every receipt photo attached to a record gets a number, R-40. The history line carries the same tag and the page it’s on. In the appendix the photo sits next to the date, mileage, work and amount, and says which line it belongs to. In the PDF both tags are links, one tap each way. Part photos stay out.

The layout follows the paper. Till slips, taller than wide and small, go two to a page. A full A4 invoice or act gets a page of its own: a one-line band with the record on top, the scan fitted below, never cropped. A two-sheet invoice takes two pages, and the band says “sheet 1 of 2”.
