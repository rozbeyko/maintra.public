Component · date picker

Our own calendar,
same sheet on iOS and Android

Replaces the system dialog. Opens from any date field as the standard Maintra sheet: grabber, Tektur headline with the chosen date, shortcut chips, month bar, grid, one gold button. Tap a day to select, the button confirms. Tap the month name for the month and year view.

Limits per field
Service, fuel, expenseUp to today. Opens on the month of the date already in the form. Shortcuts: Today, Yesterday, the last visit.
Odometer readingUp to today, and not before the previous reading’s date.
Plan item, reminderFrom today on, up to 5 years ahead. Shortcuts: In a month, In 3 months, In a year.
Doc expiryFrom today to 10 years ahead. Opens on the year grid when nothing is set.
Bought onFrom a year before the model year to today. Year first, then month, then day. “I only know the year” saves 1 Jan with a year-only flag, shown as “2019”.
Lent untilFrom today to 90 days ahead, plus a time row under the grid.
Rules
Week startFrom the phone’s region, not the app language. Monday in UA, PL, DE, FR, IT, ES; Sunday in the US, Japan, Korea, Brazil; Saturday in most Arabic regions.
NamesMonth and weekday names from the system locale (Intl), short weekdays in two letters. Digits stay Western in every language, as in the rest of the app.
RTLArabic mirrors the grid, the arrows and the plate’s slant, the same way every other cut in the app is mirrored.
Hit areaEach day is a 44 × 44 target even though the plate is 42 × 36. Five or six rows; the sheet height follows, the Set button never moves off-screen.
Screen readersGrid of buttons, one per day: “Friday, 2 October, has a record, selected”. Out-of-range days are read as unavailable. Month name is a heading and a button. Swipe left/right changes month.
MotionMonths slide 220 ms ease-out in the swipe direction; the plate snaps in 120 ms. With Reduce Motion: no slide, a 120 ms fade.
Light themeSame shapes. Plate in gold #EBA817 with #1A1814 numbers, today outline #835800, sheet #F8F6F1.
