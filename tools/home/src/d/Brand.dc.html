The mark

Mt: maintenance and tracker, drawn as a spring on a rod. Two strokes and their folds make the M, the third stroke is the t, and the t’s bar runs back through the coil. The M is calm, the t is gold: the tracker that lights up. Everything below is final unless marked otherwise.

One unitThe width of a stroke. Everything is counted in it.
Height3.5 units. Strokes lean 18°; the folds behind them stand straight, 0.55 units wide.
The bar0.66 units thick, a fifth down from the top. It starts 0.35 units left of the first stroke, ends 1 unit right of the t, and runs inside the coil: over the folds, under the strokes, which cast a hard shadow on it.
Clear spaceOne unit on every side (dashed). Nothing enters it.
Minimum20 px wide on screen with the folds; below that use the one-colour mark. 8 mm in print.

Launch · the release
Loading
Launch · 1.6 s, onceThe rod draws itself from the left (0.3 s), the coil grows out of it pressed against the end (0.3 s), holds, then lets go: every stroke travels in proportion to its distance from the first, overshoots 12%, then 5%, and rests. The t lights gold as it lands.
Loading · 2.4 s a cyclePulled slowly to its longest, let go, snaps shut past rest, two bounces, rest, then the slow pull again.
FeelImpact soft on release, a light tick when the t turns gold. Reduce Motion: fade in, no travel.
