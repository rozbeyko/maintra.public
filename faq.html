General

What is Maintra?

Maintra is an AI-powered vehicle maintenance tracker. You log your vehicles, service records, and fuel-ups; Maintra generates a personalized maintenance plan so you never miss a service interval.

Is Maintra free?

Yes — the core features are free forever. You can track one vehicle, log unlimited service records and fuel-ups, and generate 1 AI maintenance plan to get started. Pro and VIP subscriptions unlock more vehicles, more AI generations, the AI service entry, and the AI chat assistant. Our philosophy is simple: Maintra is built by a developer who fixes his own car and knows parts are expensive enough — so everything that costs nothing to run stays free, and you only pay for the features where we pay third parties, like AI processing.

Which platforms are supported?

Android and iPhone. We're available on Google Play and the Apple App Store. The iOS version is not yet available in EU App Store storefronts — EU availability is coming soon.

Does Maintra work offline?

Yes. All your data is stored locally on your device, so you can add and view records without an internet connection. Changes sync back to our servers as soon as you're online again. AI features (plan generation, chat, AI service entry) require an internet connection.

Which languages is Maintra available in?

English, Ukrainian, Polish, French, Italian, and Spanish. The app automatically picks up your device language and can be changed manually in Profile → Language & Region.

Account & Data

How do I create an account?

Open the app and sign up with your email address or with your Google account. That's it — no credit card required.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Open any vehicle, tap the menu, and choose Export as PDF. You'll get a complete service history document you can save or share.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete Account. Full instructions on the account deletion page.

Is my data shared with anyone?

We share the minimum necessary data with our backend provider (Supabase), our AI provider (Anthropic, to generate maintenance plans), and payment providers (Google Play, Apple App Store, RevenueCat). We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and we do not use tracking analytics. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does Maintra track my location?

No. Maintra does not collect your location, does not use GPS, and does not track you in any way.

AI features

How does the AI maintenance plan work?

When you tap "Generate Plan" on a vehicle, we send your vehicle details (make, model, year, mileage, fuel type, VIN, and service history summary) to Anthropic's Claude AI, which returns a personalized list of upcoming maintenance items with intervals and priorities. Responses typically take 10–30 seconds.

How accurate is the AI?

Claude is a general-purpose AI and its maintenance suggestions are based on typical intervals for your vehicle type. It may occasionally miss manufacturer-specific quirks or make minor errors. Always cross-check with your owner's manual or a qualified mechanic before performing (or skipping) any maintenance.

What are the AI limits?

AI maintenance plans:

  • Free: 1 plan generation (one-time).
  • Pro: 10 plan generations per month.
  • VIP: 30 plan generations per month.

AI service entry — describe a service in plain text and AI fills in the details:

  • Free & Pro: 1 entry (one-time).
  • VIP: 15 entries per month.

AI chat (vehicle-aware assistant) is VIP-only, with a daily limit.

Free-tier allowances are one-time; paid monthly allowances reset 30 days after first use.

What is the chat feature?

VIP subscribers can chat with a vehicle-aware AI assistant that has context about their specific vehicle. Ask it about maintenance questions, diagnostic help, or part recommendations.

Subscriptions

How much do Pro and VIP cost?

Pro is $1.99/month and VIP is $8.99/month. Prices are localized — the exact amount in your currency is shown at checkout. The paid tiers exist to cover what we pay third parties for (the AI processing); everything that costs us nothing to run stays free.

How do I subscribe?

Profile → Upgrade, or tap the Pro/VIP prompt when you hit a free-tier limit. Subscriptions are processed through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you purchased through. Android: open the Google Play Store app → tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Maintra → Cancel. iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Maintra → Cancel. Your subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you purchased through. On Android, see Google's refund policy; on iPhone, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but the app still shows Free. What do I do?

Go to Profile → Refresh subscription status. If that doesn't work, restart the app. Still stuck? Contact us.

Troubleshooting

My data isn't syncing.

Check your internet connection. If you see an offline banner at the top of the app, reconnect and the app will sync automatically. You can also force a sync by pulling down to refresh on any list screen.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link.

Still need help?

Get in touch — we usually respond within 48 hours.
