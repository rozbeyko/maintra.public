01Garage

Your car on the wall

Open Maintra and the first thing you see is your car: a real photo in a polaroid, the odometer stamped on it like on film, and the one number that matters right now. Below it, only what's late or close. Everything else is one tap away.

Try Tap the photo of Paj.

02Log it

Snap the receipt. That's the job.

A garage invoice, a fuel slip, a receipt for parts, in any language. Maintra reads it and turns it into records: parts, labour, cost, mileage. You check every line before it's saved, and a misread costs you nothing. Hands black from work? Hold the plus and just say what you did.

Try Press Photo, then the shutter.

03Plan

Knows what's due, and says why

Maintra builds a plan from your car and your mileage: what's late, what's soon, what can wait. Every line says when and why. Mark a job done and the countdown starts over. Before a long drive, it checks the car for you.

Try Open a job, mark it done.

04Money

What the car really costs

Every hryvnia, euro or dollar in one place, converted at the rate of the day you spent it. Driving and owning apart: per kilometre, per month, since you bought it. A fine reminds you before the half price runs out.

Try Scroll down the money screen.

05MapNew

Offline maps that count your kilometres

Download your region once and drive without signal. Maintra leads the way, counts the trip and adds the kilometres to the odometer, so the plan stays right. Speeds and the fastest stretch, if you're curious. The track never leaves your phone.

Try Press Route, then Go.

06OBD scansNew

Plug in. Read the car.

A cheap OBD adapter under the dash and Maintra reads the codes, the freeze frame and live data, explains them in plain words and keeps every scan in the car's history. Compare before and after a fix, or send it to your mechanic.

Try Tap Scan now.

07Hand overNew

Lend it. Share it. Sell it with the history.

Lending Paj till Sunday? Your mate gets the car on their phone, logs fuel and kilometres, and when the keys come back you see their entries kept apart. Drive it together with your partner. Or sell it, and the whole service history moves to the new owner. A car with its history sells better.

Try Tap the keys at the top.

08MechanicNew

A link instead of a phone call

Send your mechanic a link: what's late, your note about that knock, fluids and part numbers. They open it in a browser, no app and no account, and send back what they did. You decide what goes into the history. The car sheet keeps oil specs, filter numbers and tyre sizes on one page.

Try Tap Send link.

09Tyres and papers

Tyres by age. Papers under lock.

Tread on every wheel, rotation, and the date the rubber was made: after six years it hardens, whatever the tread says. Insurance and registration live in a vault encrypted on your phone, with a key only you hold. No recovery button. Inconvenient on purpose.

Try Open the set on the car.

10Make it yoursNew

Your garage. Your look. Your language.

Dark, light or like your phone. Then the themes: Red light for night drives, Field, Blueprint, Logbook, Rose. A theme changes the background and the accent; red still means late. And the whole app now speaks eleven languages, right to left too.

Why it's worth it

Made for the people who do the work

No feature list for the sake of it. Each of these came from someone who lost a receipt, a record or a weekend.

  • The book is free

    Records, fuel, plan, reminders, stats, export. Free on every plan, for good. You pay only for what costs money to run: the AI.

  • Works without signal

    Everything lands on your phone first. Underground parking, a garage in a village, a mountain pass: it works, and syncs later.

  • No ads. No tracking.

    Car photos and trips stay on the phone that took them. Nothing is sold, nothing follows you around.

  • Your data, your file

    JSON, CSV or a PDF service book, on every plan, whenever you like. Delete the account in the app and it's gone.

  • AI never guesses quietly

    Whatever it reads, you check before it's saved. What it can't read stays empty, and where every value came from is marked.

  • Papers under your key

    Documents are encrypted on the phone before they're uploaded. Maintra never holds the key.

  • Tyres by date, not just tread

    Rubber hardens after six years. Maintra reads the DOT code and tells you, whatever the tread says.

  • Your currency, the rate of the day

    50 currencies, 45 countries. A receipt from 2019 counts at the 2019 rate.

  • Cars, vans, motorbikes

    Petrol, diesel, LPG, hybrid or electric. One garage for all of them.

  • Switching is free

    Bring your history from a spreadsheet or another app. No AI in the import, so the numbers match line for line.

  • Phone, tablet, wrist

    iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, widgets on the home and lock screen. Siri and Apple Watch for quick entries.

  • A person, not a call centre

    Write to support and the person who built it answers.

Who made it

Built in a garage, not an office

I'm ROK. I keep a 2008 Pajero on the road with my own hands, and Maintra started because I kept forgetting which oil went in, how much, and when. Receipts in the glovebox, notes in a spreadsheet, chats with AI: it all got lost.

So I built the book I wanted. More than a thousand drivers use it today, and 2.0 is what they told me: calmer screens, less typing, and the things I was missing in my own garage. Maps that work in the woods, a scan when the check-engine light comes on, a link for the mechanic.

You remember the big repairs without an app. It's the small things that get lost: which filter exactly, how much went in, when it was last done.

I didn't want to build another subscription. Anything that costs nothing to run stays free.

ROK, Kyrylo Rozbeiko
