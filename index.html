Questions?

A few of the most common ones — the full FAQ has more.

Does Maintra work offline?

Yes — all your data is stored locally and syncs when you reconnect. Only AI features need internet.

Does it track my location?

No. Maintra does not collect location, does not use GPS, and does not track you in any way.

Can I export my service history?

Yes — open a vehicle, tap the menu, choose Export as PDF. Hand it to a buyer or mechanic.

iPhone version?

Yes — Maintra is on the App Store. EU storefronts are coming soon.
