Coolant hose is 5 days late. Can I still drive to Lviv this weekend?
You can, but check it cold first. Squeeze the hose: if it feels soft or there is white crust at the clamps, change it before the motorway.A hose for the 3.0 V6 is about 600–900 ₴ and it is a 40-minute job with the coolant drain. On the way, watch the temperature gauge and carry a litre of coolant.
